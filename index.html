Tap in the green. Support page.

How to play

A pointer sweeps around a ring. Tap anywhere when it is inside the green zone. Every hit flips the direction, speeds the pointer up and shrinks the zone. Miss and the run ends. Hit the center for a PERFECT and chain hits to build a streak multiplier.

Frequently asked questions

How do I continue a run after a miss?
Watch a short video or spend 100 coins on the game over screen. You resume with the same score and streak.

How do I remove ads?
Open Store and buy Remove ads once. It stays on your account. If you reinstall or change phone, tap Restore purchases in the Store.

My purchase did not arrive.
Open Store and tap Restore purchases. If a coin pack is still missing, contact us below with the date and your Apple receipt.

How do I turn off sound, vibration or reminders?
Open the menu (top left of Home), then Settings.

Is my progress saved online?
No. Progress is saved on your device only, so deleting the app removes it.
