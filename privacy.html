Privacy Policy

Perfect Ring. Last updated: October 12, 2026

Perfect Ring ("the app") is a game by Mohammed Shahid Nawaz. Contact: github.com/PexefaBuilds/perfect-ring-support/issues.

What stays on your device

Your best score, coins, level progress, daily reward streak, whether you removed ads, sound and vibration settings, and the time your next free-coins video is ready are saved on your device only. The daily reward uses your device clock; no time or date is sent to us. The app has no accounts and does not send this data to us.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules up to two reminders on your device (a daily gift reminder and a come-back reminder). They are local notifications created by the app on your phone; they use no server, and nothing about them is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Ads

The app shows optional rewarded video ads and a small banner ad at the bottom of screens, through Google AdMob. Buying Remove Ads turns off the banner. To serve and measure ads, Google and its ad partners may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • ad interactions and diagnostics

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, the app asks for your consent before ads load, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases (remove ads, coin packs) through Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by the store. We never see your card or payment details; the store tells the app only whether a purchase succeeded. "Restore purchases" asks the store which items your account owns.

What we do not collect

Name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or any account data. We do not run our own servers or send your game data to us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age set by your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: open an issue at github.com/PexefaBuilds/perfect-ring-support/issues.
